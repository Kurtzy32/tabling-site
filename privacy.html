Privacy Policy

Last updated November 9, 2026

Tabling turns your weekly brain dump into a planned week on your calendar. It's built so your notes and plans stay yours: Tabling has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The developer never receives any of your data.

What Tabling stores, and where

  • Your notes, day notes, tasks and settings are stored on your device. If you turn on iCloud sync, they're also stored in your own private iCloud account, so your iPad, iPhone and Mac stay in step. Only you can read your iCloud data; the developer can't.
  • Background images you choose for themes are copied into the app on your device (and your iCloud, if sync is on).

To delete everything, delete the app. If you use iCloud sync, you can also remove Tabling's data in Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

Calendar

With your permission, Tabling reads the calendars you choose, on your device, to show your week and find free time. It adds events only to the one calendar you pick as your Tabling calendar, and only when you tap Approve. It also updates or removes events it created when you mark a task done, move it, or remove it. Calendar data isn't sent anywhere by Tabling; your calendars sync with their own services (such as Google) through your device's account settings, as they already do.

Microphone and speech

When you tap the dictation button, Tabling uses the microphone to turn your speech into text on your device. Listening stops when you tap stop. Audio isn't recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

Apple Intelligence

"Process My Week" uses Apple's on-device language model to find tasks in your notes. Your notes are processed on your device and aren't sent to the developer or any third party. On devices without Apple Intelligence, Tabling turns each line into a task itself, also on your device.

Photos

If you choose your own background image, Tabling only sees the image you pick in the system photo picker. It doesn't access the rest of your library.

Children

Tabling doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.
