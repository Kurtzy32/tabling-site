Tabling

Give your thoughts a home

Write down everything on your mind. Tabling finds the tasks, plans them into the free time on your calendar, and adds them only after you approve.

Getting started

  1. Add your calendar account (such as Google) to your device in Settings → Calendar → Accounts.
  2. Create a calendar named Tabling (on calendar.google.com, for a Google account), then pick it in Tabling's Settings under Write To.
  3. Set your work hours and sleep in Settings → Schedule.
  4. Write your notes, tap Process My Week, then Plan Full Week, and approve the times you like.

Common questions

My Google calendars don't show up.
Tabling sees the calendars on your device. Add your Google account in Settings → Calendar → Accounts, and allow Tabling full calendar access in Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars.

Approved tasks aren't in Google Calendar yet.
Your device syncs with Google on its own schedule, usually within a few minutes.

"Process My Week" isn't available.
It uses Apple Intelligence, which needs a supported device with Apple Intelligence turned on. Without it, the button reads Make Tasks from My Notes and turns each line into a task you can edit.

Does Tabling ever change my other calendars?
No. It only adds events to the calendar you choose, and only when you tap Approve.
